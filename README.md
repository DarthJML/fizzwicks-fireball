# Fizzwick's Fireball

A small browser game: fling firebolts and fireballs, survive the waves, and grow your spell constellation between waves.

**Play:** https://poptartsmmmgood117-bit.github.io/fizzwicks-fireball/

Controls (desktop): WASD or arrow keys move; mouse aims; hold left click to auto-fire Firebolt; right click or Space casts Fireball; Q casts Frost Nova and E casts Ward once unlocked; T opens the spell tree; 1/2/3 choose draft cards.

Controls (phone / X player card): Touch the left half and drag the floating joystick to move. Tap the right half to aim and fire a Firebolt, or hold and drag to auto-fire. Move and fire with two fingers. Use the big FIREBALL button, or tap/hold the smaller FIREBOLT button to target the nearest enemy. FROST NOVA and WARD stay visible but locked until drafted; active rings show cooldown. Tap ✦ at top-right for the spell tree; tap a draft card to choose it.

It's one self-contained HTML file with no install and no tracking.
